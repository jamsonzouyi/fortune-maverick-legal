Privacy Policy

Fortune Maverick · Last updated: 2026-10-03
Short version: this game does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no servers, makes no network requests, and requests no Android permissions. Everything you do stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Fortune Maverick ("the Game") is developed and published by an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at the contact address in section 8.

2. What we collect

Nothing. Specifically:

  • The Game makes no network requests. It works fully offline.
  • The Game requests no Android permissions — not even INTERNET.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or social login.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, device identifiers, or advertising ID.

3. What is stored on your device

Your game progress (virtual cash, holdings, project records, settings and in-game statistics) is saved locally on your device only, using the standard web storage of the app's built-in browser component (localStorage / sessionStorage).

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it. The Game includes an "Export save" feature that copies your save as text so you can back it up yourself. If you use it, you decide where that text goes.

The Game also keeps an anonymous in-memory event log for its own debugging panel. This log lives only in memory, is capped in size, and is discarded when the Game closes. You can view and clear it from the in-game settings.

4. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected at all, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request deletion of.

5. Financial disclaimer

The Game is a simulation. All money, securities, cryptocurrencies, funds, projects and assets in it are virtual and fictional. They:

  • cannot be withdrawn,
  • cannot be exchanged for real money or anything of real-world value,
  • are not connected to any real brokerage, exchange or trading venue.

The Game does not provide investment advice, and nothing in it should be relied upon as such. Market behaviour in the Game is synthetic and does not represent any real instrument.

6. Permissions

The Game declares no Android permissions. If a future version adds advertising (see section 7), this policy will be updated before that version is released.

7. Future changes

If we later introduce advertising or any feature that changes what data is processed, we will:

  • update this policy and its "last updated" date,
  • update the Google Play Data safety declaration accordingly, and
  • where required by law, ask for your consent in-app before any such processing begins.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: z-yi@foxmail.com
